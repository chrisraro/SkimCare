1Definitions

“You” means the person about to sign. “The document” means the agreement in your hand: a freelance contract, a service agreement, an installment or loan agreement. “SkimCare” means an app that reads the document with you, on your phone, and points to its source every time it says something.

SkimCare note
Explained from sourcePlain version: a reading companion, not a lawyer.

2How SkimCare reads

You scan the pages or import a PDF. SkimCare turns them into text, asks you to confirm anything it could not read cleanly, then finds the terms that matter: amounts, due dates, fees, renewals, penalties. Each finding is shown beside the sentence it came from, so you can check it yourself.

Installment agreement, page 2 Sample agreement, fictional
A late payment fee of five percent (5%) of the unpaid installment shall be charged for every month or fraction thereof that the installment remains unpaid.
Needs attentionPaying even one day late adds 5% of that installment, and the fee repeats every month it stays unpaid.
The Seller may adjust the monthly rate upon notice to the Buyer.
Needs clarificationThe document does not say how much notice, or how far the rate can change. Ask before signing.
Each installment is due on the fifth (5th) day of every month.
Explained from sourcePay by the 5th of each month.
What if I pay 10 days late on a ₱4,500 installment? ₱225 fee 5% × ₱4,500, for one month or part of a month. Worked out by fixed rules, not by the AI.

3Where your document goes

Nowhere. Text recognition, reading and explanations run on the phone. There is no account to create, no cloud copy, and no upload. Once the reading model is installed, SkimCare works in airplane mode. Your reviews are saved privately on the device.

SkimCare note
Explained from sourceNo internet needed to read a document. Setup downloads the model once.

4What SkimCare will not do

SkimCare will not tell you whether to sign. It will not call a clause legal or illegal, fair or unfair.

It will not guess a number the document does not state. When a term is missing, it says so: “insufficient information.”

It is a document comprehension and legal-information aid, not legal advice. For advice, talk to a lawyer.
